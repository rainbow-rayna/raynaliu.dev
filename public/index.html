Rayna Liu

I explore the evolving ways AI can enrich human interaction, and design products that help people know themselves more deeply. I believe the more people understand themselves, the more unmet needs surface — and each of those needs is an opportunity.

I'm a creator at heart — I love building things that solve real problems. With AI advancing so fast, I'm drawn to exploring new possibilities at the intersection of Human × AI.

One thing I find curious: the speed of AI has quietly increased anxiety, insecurity, and overwhelm for a lot of people. I felt that deeply myself, coming from a non-technical, no-code background. (Fun fact: I studied Econ & Art in college!) My hope is that my projects can ease some of that unease — acting as a bridge connecting me with others thinking through the same questions. Hope you enjoy exploring!

Projects

Memories — AI Conversation-to-Scrapbook

React Three Fiber · Three.js · GSAP

Photos pile up, but the feeling behind them fades — and journaling, the habit that would keep it, usually stalls at a blank page. Tell Memories about a moment the way you'd text a friend. It writes the entry in your voice, adds a line of reflection that fits the feeling, and illustrates the page — kept in a 3D album you flip through like a real scrapbook. A few messages replace a writing session, and every moment becomes a finished page worth coming back to.

Moodboard — AI-Tagged Visual Inspiration for Creators

React · Express · Gemini · Postgres

Creators save screenshots constantly, but a folder of them can't say what makes each one work — and putting a visual style into words is a skill of its own. Pin a screenshot to a day and it comes back as a Polaroid tagged with the design language it speaks — Brutalist, Modular Grid, Muted Earth Tones — across style, layout, type, colour and material, ready to copy into a search or a brief. Each week is its own board. References arrive already named, so it's easier to find more like them, brief a collaborator, or put words to your own taste.
